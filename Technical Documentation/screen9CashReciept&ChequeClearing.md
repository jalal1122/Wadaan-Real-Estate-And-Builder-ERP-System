If Screen 8 is where you track who owes you money, Screen 9 is where the physical money actually crosses your desk.
In real estate, clients rarely hand over Rs. 5,000,000 in raw cash. They use Cheques, Pay Orders, or Online Transfers. If you instantly record a cheque as "Cash in Bank," but the cheque bounces three days later, your accounting is completely ruined. Screen 9 is designed to protect Wadaan's bank ledgers from this exact problem.
1. What You See (The Interface)
The screen is split into two distinct areas: the Entry Form and the Waiting Room.
The Entry Form (Top): A fast, clean input area where you log the payment the exact second a client walks into the office. You select the Client (e.g., Zain - Plot 45), enter the Amount, and choose the Payment Method (Cash, Cheque, or Online).
The Waiting Room (Bottom): A dashboard grid listing all the cheques and online transfers that you have received in your hands, but that have not yet officially cleared the bank.
2. The Cheque Lock (Mandatory Paper Trail)
When a client hands you a payment, the system adapts based on what they gave you.
Cash Payments: If they give you cash, the money goes instantly into the "Office Safe" bucket on Screen 1.
Cheques / Transfers: If you select Cheque or Online Transfer, the system instantly generates a mandatory "Bank Name & Cheque Number" text box. You cannot click "Save" until you type this in. This guarantees you never have a mysterious Rs. 500,000 payment in the system without knowing exactly which piece of paper it belongs to.
3. The Cheque "Waiting Room" (The Safety Net)
This is the most protective feature of the revenue module.
How it Works: When you log a Meezan Bank cheque from Zain, the system does not put that money into your Wadaan Bank Account yet. It places it in the Waiting Room.
The Customer Experience: Even though the money isn't in your bank yet, the system instantly updates Zain’s Khaata on Screen 8 to say "Paid (Pending Clearance)." This turns off his overdue alarms so you stop calling him for recovery, but keeps your actual bank balance safe.
4. The Action Buttons (Clear vs. Bounce)
Every two or three days, you open your real-world bank app on your phone, compare it to the Screen 9 Waiting Room, and take action.
Action A: "Mark as Cleared": If the bank app shows the money has successfully landed, you click this button. The system instantly moves the money out of the Waiting Room and into Wadaan's Live Bank Balance.
Action B: "Bounce / Dishonor": If the cheque bounces, you click this button. The system simply deletes the pending payment and reinstates Zain's red "Overdue Debt" status on Screen 8 so you know to call him immediately. It does not auto-apply any complex penalty fees, keeping the reversal clean and simple.
5. The Dual-Copy Receipt Voucher (Physical Proof)
Clients will not hand over massive payments without immediate, official proof.
The Feature: The precise second you click "Save Payment" on a new receipt, a "Print Receipt" button appears.
The Output: It instantly generates a highly professional, branded A4 PDF divided exactly in half.
Top Half (Wadaan's Copy): Contains the date, the amount, the cheque number, what exactly the payment was for (e.g., "Villa 5 - Installment 3"), and a signature line. You file this.
Bottom Half (Client's Copy): An exact duplicate for the client to take home for their own peace of mind.
6. Business Rules & Guardrails
The Advance Wallet Link: If the money you received was a "Mobilization Advance" for a construction project, the moment you click "Mark as Cleared," the system automatically injects those funds into the Customer Wallet (built in Screen 8). You can now safely use that cleared money to buy cement and steel.
The Cash Reversal Lock: You can "Bounce" a cheque, but you cannot "Bounce" a cash payment. If you made a mistake with a cash receipt, you must use a proper reversal process to ensure the cash drawer audit trail remains intact.
Screen 9 ensures that every single rupee entering Wadaan is receipted, tracked, and verified by the bank before you ever consider it "safe."
