Here is the fully detailed, non-technical blueprint for Screen 3: Live Trial Balance & Balance Sheet (The Financial X-Ray).
Think of Screen 3 as the ultimate "truth teller" for Wadaan Real Estate. You do zero data entry on this screen. It simply reads everything you have done on all the other screens (bills paid, cash received, projects built) and instantly translates it into standard, professional financial reports.
If you ever need to show a bank, an investor, or a tax consultant how the business is doing, this is the screen you open.
1. What You See (The Interface)
The screen is designed to be a clean, print-ready dashboard with three main tabs at the top, allowing you to flip between Wadaan’s core financial statements.
The Look: A crisp, organized financial document. No chaotic buttons or data entry boxes.
The Date Filter: At the top, you can select a specific timeframe (e.g., "This Month," "This Year," or "Custom Dates" like Jan 1 to June 30). The numbers instantly recalculate based on the dates you pick.
The Export Button: A prominent "Download PDF" button that takes exactly what is on your screen and turns it into a branded Wadaan report ready for WhatsApp or printing.
2. The Three Master Reports (What They Tell You)
When you click through the tabs, the system automatically builds three distinct reports to give you the full picture of the business:
Tab 1: The Trial Balance (The Mathematical Proof) This is simply a list of every single bucket from Screen 1 that has money in it, split into two columns (Debits and Credits). Its only job is to prove that Wadaan’s accounting is flawless. The total at the very bottom of both columns will always perfectly match.
Tab 2: The Income Statement / P&L (Office Profitability) This shows your Revenue (property sales, commissions) minus your Expenses (office rent, utility bills, salaries). (Note: The cost of cement and bricks for ongoing projects does NOT show up as a loss here; those are safely tracked as Assets until the project is sold).
Tab 3: The Balance Sheet (The Net Worth) This is a snapshot of Wadaan’s overall health today. It shows everything Wadaan Owns (cash, bank balance, unsold plots, half-built plazas) matched perfectly against everything Wadaan Owes (unpaid vendor bills, client escrow money, advances).
3. The "Drill-Down" Feature (Interactive Investigation)
These aren't just dead numbers on a page. The entire report is interactive.
If you are looking at the Income Statement and see that "Marketing Expense" says Rs. 150,000, and you think, "Wait, how did I spend that much on marketing?"...
You simply click on the number "150,000."
A window instantly slides out showing the exact history—every individual Facebook Ad receipt or billboard payment from Screen 5 that adds up to that Rs. 150,000. You never have to go digging through paper files to find out where the money went.
4. Business Rules & Guardrails
Because this is a reporting screen, the safety rules are simple but absolute.
The Read-Only Rule: You cannot type, edit, delete, or change any number on this screen. It is physically impossible. If a number is wrong here, it means a bill or receipt was entered wrong on another screen. You must go fix the source, and this screen will automatically correct itself.
The Real-Time Sync: There is no "refresh" button or "end of day batching." If you are looking at this screen on your laptop, and you use your phone to quickly log a Rs. 50,000 customer payment on Screen 9, the Cash total on Screen 3 instantly jumps by Rs. 50,000 right in front of your eyes.
The Perpetual Balance Lock: Because of the guardrails we built into Screen 2 (General Journal) and Screen 5 (Bills), it is mathematically impossible for the Balance Sheet to ever be out of balance.
Screen 3 proves that your business is healthy, organized, and running like a corporate enterprise, even as a solo operator.
