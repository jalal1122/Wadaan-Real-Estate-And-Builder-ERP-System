1. What You See (The Interface)
The entry point is designed to be completely private, clean, and professional.
The Look: A minimalist page with the Wadaan Real Estate logo centered on the screen.
The Inputs: Only two text boxes: Email and Password.
The Actions: A "Login" button and a "Forgot Password?" link.
The "No Sign-Up" Rule: Because this is Wadaan’s private financial system, random visitors cannot create an account. There is no "Register" or "Sign Up" button. Your single master account is pre-built into the system from day one.
2. What the System Remembers (The User Record)
Behind the scenes, the system securely tracks a few key details about your account to keep it safe.
Email Address: Your unique login ID.
Scrambled Password: The system never saves your actual password in plain text. It heavily scrambles it so that even the software developers cannot see what your password is.
Failed Attempts: A silent counter that tracks how many times someone typed the wrong password.
Account Status: Tracks whether your login is currently "Active" or temporarily "Locked."
Last Login Time: Records the exact date and time you last successfully entered the system, so you always know when your account was used.
3. Business Rules & Security Guardrails
Since this software holds Wadaan's bank balances and client data, the login screen acts like a bank vault door with strict automatic rules.
The Anti-Guessing Lock (Brute-Force Protection): If someone finds your login page and tries to guess your password, they won't get far. If an incorrect password is entered 5 times in a row, the system instantly locks the account for 30 minutes.
The Auto-Logout Rule (Session Expiry): If you are working in the office, leave your laptop open, and go home for the night, your data is still safe. The system is programmed to automatically log you out after 12 hours. The next morning, you must log in fresh.
The Invisible Bouncer: You cannot bypass this screen. If an employee or outsider somehow gets the direct web link to Screen 4 (Projects) or Screen 7 (Payments) and pastes it into their browser, the system will instantly block them and force them back to this login screen.
